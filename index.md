## About Me

I am Yunpeng Li, an Associate Professor at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my Ph.D. from the same institute and was selected for an institute talent program. My research spans the full pipeline of large-model systems: multimodal and multilingual understanding at the input layer, agentic intelligence and domain enablement at the application layer, and trustworthy and secure AI across the entire lifecycle. Security is the central thread of my work, while I also study broader problems in model understanding, generation, retrieval, and agents.

My core research focuses on the reliability and security of LLMs and agents, including watermarking and provenance, backdoor attacks and defenses, secure RAG, vulnerability discovery, cyber threat intelligence, and risk-aware decision support. Beyond security, I work on multimodal understanding, cross-lingual retrieval, controllable generation, narrative intelligence, agent orchestration, and world models, with the goal of turning foundation-model capabilities into dependable systems for cybersecurity and other knowledge-intensive domains.

I have led or contributed to multiple national and provincial research programs, including National 2030 Critical Infrastructure, National Key Research and Development, National Science and Technology Major Projects, and the Joint Key Fund of the National Natural Science Foundation of China. I have published more than 50 papers in international conferences and journals, including ICML, AAAI, NeurIPS, SIGIR, ACL, and related venues.

Beyond academic research, related technologies have been deployed across multiple national ministries and government agencies, and have successively served major national-level settings, including the Winter Olympics, the National Two Sessions, and the Commemorative Activities Marking the 80th Anniversary of the Victory in the Chinese People's War of Resistance Against Japanese Aggression and the World Anti-Fascist War, providing strong support for cybersecurity assurance. I also led the development of a national-level regulatory operations platform, which has become a core system for analysis, risk perception, and early-warning support.

## Research Interests

- **Multimodal and Multilingual Understanding:** studying input-side understanding and representation across text, images, code, and network data, including cross-lingual retrieval and narrative modeling.
- **LLM Agents and Domain Enablement:** developing application-side agents, orchestration, tool use, and world models for cybersecurity and other knowledge-intensive tasks.
- **Trustworthy and Secure AI (Core Focus):** securing the full lifecycle of large-model systems through watermarking and provenance, factual and secure RAG, backdoor robustness, vulnerability discovery, and risk-aware decision-making.
